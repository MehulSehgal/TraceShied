How to export from Instagram
  1. Open Instagram, then Settings, then Accounts Center, then Your information and permissions, then Download your information. Menu names can change.
  2. Choose Messages and the JSON format, then request the download.
  3. Unzip the file and open messages/inbox/your-chat/message_1.json.
  4. Upload that file above.

Safety guidance

    
    
      
  • Do not delete concerning messages. Keep the original conversation and note relevant dates.
    • 
      
  • Tell someone you trust, such as a friend, family member, counselor, or campus support service.
    • 
      
  • Consider reporting the account on its platform and contacting appropriate campus or local authorities.
    • 
      
  • If you are in India, the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal is cybercrime.gov.in; the cybercrime helpline is 1930. Verify current contact details and procedures.

If you feel in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or someone who can help you right away. This prototype is not an emergency service.

  






  
ABOUT TRACESHIELD
Understand the signals. Keep the context.
A transparent guide to what the score means, and what it cannot tell you.

  
How the score is computed

    
The heuristic adds points for five signals: repeated contact after a refusal (+2 when there are at least 3 messages), night-time messages (+1 when more than 30% are between 00:00 and 04:59), an increasing severity trend (+1), surveillance-type remarks (+2), and threats (+3). The maximum possible total is 9 points.

    
Scores of 0–2 are Low, 3–4 are Elevated, and 5 or more are High. These thresholds organize patterns for review; they are not probabilities.

  

  
Limitations

    
    
      
  • This is a prototype using fixed text patterns and heuristic rules. It may miss harmful behavior or flag context that is benign.
    • 
      
  • Language, sarcasm, surrounding context, and messages outside the supplied conversation can affect interpretation.
    • 
      
  • The result is a risk assessment, not a legal verdict, proof, or substitute for professional advice. Review the original conversation and use your judgment.
    • 
      
  • Analysis runs in your browser unless you explicitly choose the optional local backend. The live monitor is simulated and does not read other pages.
    • 
    

  

  
TraceShield gives a risk assessment, not a legal verdict. This prototype uses rules plus a Hinglish knowledge base. LLM labelling and vector retrieval are optional and not enabled in this demo. Test data is synthetic.
